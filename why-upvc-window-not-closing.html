Why Is My UPVC Window Not Closing Properly?

A UPVC window may fail to close because of alignment, hinge wear, debris in the frame or a hardware issue. Check for visible obstructions without forcing the handle. If the sash still catches, share photos and describe where it touches the frame.

What we can help you check

  • Identify the window or door type before requesting help.
  • Take clear photos where it is safe to do so.
  • Contact a technician if the fault persists or affects security.

How to make an enquiry

Tell us your Hyderabad locality, the type of UPVC window or door, and what is happening. Clear photos can help explain the issue. The exact fault, parts required, service availability and quotation are confirmed after discussion or inspection.
